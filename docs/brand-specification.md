# BZaudio Brand Specification

## Brand idea

**BZaudio — Professional Audio. Made Easier.**

BZaudio is a practical, professional, approachable audio company. It sells products from established audio manufacturers and helps customers select, install, and support complete systems.

## Positioning

BZaudio is not positioned as a speaker or mixer manufacturer. It is a multi-brand audio retailer and systems service company supported by useful web tools.

## Audiences

- Beginners buying their first serious audio setup.
- Churches, schools, community organizations, and venues.
- DJs, bands, production teams, and live sound technicians.
- Businesses that need installed background music, paging, or presentation audio.

## Business areas

1. **BZaudio Supply:** product discovery, sales, and supplier-based fulfillment.
2. **BZaudio Installation:** consultation, system design, installation, upgrades, and support.
3. **BZaudio SoundMatch:** guided cross-brand equipment selection and package creation.
4. **BZaudio Tools:** practical supporting calculators and reference tools.
5. **Customer System Profiles:** planned records for equipment, documents, diagrams, and service history.

## Brand personality

- Knowledgeable, clear, and trustworthy.
- Technical when necessary, never needlessly complicated.
- Helpful to beginners without talking down to professionals.
- Transparent about assumptions, product availability, and limitations.

## Experience principles

- Organize shopping around customer needs as well as product categories and brands.
- Explain why an item is recommended.
- Show required accessories and compatibility considerations.
- Make quote and installation requests easy to find.
- Never present unverified stock, pricing, specifications, or compatibility as fact.

## Differentiator

SoundMatch turns a customer's venue, use case, budget, and existing equipment into three understandable proposed system packages: Essential, Recommended, and Professional. Each recommendation should explain its reasoning and flag what needs human verification.

## Scope guardrails

- Do not manufacture or imply BZaudio-branded speakers, mixers, or other hardware.
- Do not promise universal compatibility.
- Do not imply that an automated recommendation replaces professional engineering.
- Start with a focused web experience; expand only when business operations justify it.
